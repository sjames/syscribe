Requirements for presenting a package's membership: the member list is generated from the
directory on every surface rather than maintained by hand in `_index.md` prose, which drifts
(GH #120). Governed by `ADR-SYS-PKG-001` (`Decisions::PackageMembershipADR`). The member requirements are
listed by `syscribe show Requirements::PackageMembership` (generated from this directory — not
maintained here).
