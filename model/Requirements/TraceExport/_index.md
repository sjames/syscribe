Requirements for the single-document traceability export: one JSON report of every requirement's
derivation, satisfaction and verification, optionally projected onto a product-line
configuration and emitted in a declared sort order, for coverage checks and external tools.

All requirements derive from `REQ-TRS-TREX-000` and are governed by `ADR-SYS-TREX-001`
(`Decisions::TraceExportADR`).

The member requirements are listed by `syscribe show Requirements::TraceExport` (generated from
this directory — not maintained here).
