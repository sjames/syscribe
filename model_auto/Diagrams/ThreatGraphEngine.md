The threat analysis of the Engine ECU **derived from the model**, left to right: threat scenario, the
damage it can cause, the asset harmed, the cybersecurity goal that protects it and the security
controls that implement the goal. Threats carry their risk under the project's configured risk method
(`[cyber]` in `.syscribe.toml`) as colour, a goal no control implements carries a `W802` badge, and a
threat no goal treats a `no goal` badge.
