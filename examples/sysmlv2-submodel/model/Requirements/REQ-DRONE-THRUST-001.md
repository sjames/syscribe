The drone assembly shall provide a thrust-to-weight ratio of at least 2:1 at maximum takeoff mass, across every supported rotor configuration.

## Rationale

Satisfied by the SysML v2 `Drone` part def via `satisfy Requirements::'REQ-DRONE-THRUST-001';` — the Syscribe-qualified-name form of `REQ-TRS-SYSMLV2-003`, as opposed to `REQ-DRONE-ENDUR-001`'s quoted-id form.
