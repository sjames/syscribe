Leaf tier of the worked example (`REQ-TRS-HPLE-000..005`, `ADR-SYS-HPLE-001`): a cell-chemistry
product line, developed and versioned entirely independently of whoever eventually consolidates it.
It declares no `subConfigurations:` — a leaf tier has no lower tiers of its own to consolidate,
exactly as `ADR-SYS-HPLE-001` describes ("naturally empty or absent at a leaf tier").
