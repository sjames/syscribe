The mandatory STM32 controller (REQ-GTI-SYS-070). Part: STM32G474RE, Cortex-M4F at 170 MHz, 512 kB flash, 128 kB RAM, HRTIM with 184 ps resolution, 5 ADCs, 7 comparators and 4 op-amps. Selected in ADR-GTI-MCU-001. Clock and window watchdogs and the vendor Class B library support IEC 60730 / UL 1998 self-test.
