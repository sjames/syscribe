name: "Independent MPPT trackers"
status: approved
reqDomain: system
verificationMethod: test
derivedFrom:
  - REQ-GTI-STK-002
breakdownAdr: ADR-GTI-REQ-001
---

The inverter **shall** provide as many independently tracked inputs as the product configuration selects (2 or 3), each with two string inputs, such that the operating voltage of one tracker does not constrain another.
