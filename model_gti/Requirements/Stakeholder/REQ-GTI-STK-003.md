---
type: Requirement
id: REQ-GTI-STK-003
name: "Protect people and property from electric shock, arc fire and unintended energisation of the grid"
status: approved
reqDomain: system
verificationMethod: test
---

The inverter **shall** not endanger installers, owners, firefighters or utility workers: no unintended energisation of a de-energised grid, no unmitigated DC arc or leakage hazard, no thermal runaway.
