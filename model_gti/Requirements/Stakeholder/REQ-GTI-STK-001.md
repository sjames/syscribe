---
type: Requirement
id: REQ-GTI-STK-001
name: "Export solar energy to the utility grid in conformance with the interconnection rules of the region of sale"
status: approved
reqDomain: system
verificationMethod: test
---

The inverter **shall** convert PV power to AC and export it to the utility grid in conformance with the interconnection requirements of the region in which it is sold (United States, India), so that the utility accepts it for parallel operation.
