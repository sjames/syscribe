---
type: Requirement
id: REQ-GTI-STK-002
name: "Harvest the maximum energy from several high-voltage PV strings, including mismatched and partially shaded strings"
status: approved
reqDomain: system
verificationMethod: test
---

The plant owner **shall** obtain the maximum available energy from the array: strings of different length, orientation or shading must not reduce one another's output, and shading must not leave the system on a poor operating point.
